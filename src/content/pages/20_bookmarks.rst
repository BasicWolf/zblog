* `Cultivating Minds: The Psychological Consequences of Rice versus Wheat Farming (2024)
  <https://marginalrevolution.com/marginalrevolution/2024/04/cultivating-minds-the-psychological-consequences-of-rice-versus-wheat-farming.html>`_
  by  Alex Tabarrok.
  |br|
  A review of how cultivating rice results in more collectivistic culture, compared
  to cultivating wheat, which results in more individualistic culture.
  In short - cultivating rice requires a coordinated effort of many people,
  while growing wheat requires less labour and coordination.
  | #culture #rice #wheat #collectivism #individualism
