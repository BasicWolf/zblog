* Poker Decaffeinato - 27,80 € / kg - 500g
  Poker Decaffeinato is a decaffeinated 50 % Arabica / 50 % Robusta coffee blend from Italian coffee roastery Pera. Poker contains coffee beans from Central America and Africa.
  Me: Sweetness of Robusta dominates.

* Lucaffé Decaffeinato - Roast: medium - Chocolatty, Nutty; 100% Arabica - 42,71 € / kg - 700g
  This caffeine free alternative from Lucaffé is roasted of the sweetest and creamiest coffees. The goal is to be able to offer the same espresso enjoyment as the caffeinated options. A soft and full-bodied espresso.
  The coffee is decaffeinated using the methylene chloride method. During the decaffeination process the coffee beans come into direct contact with the solvent, which is then removed through vaporisation.
  Me: Very strong Arabica taste. Great replacement for normal coffee.
