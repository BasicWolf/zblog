Privacy Policy
##############

:status: hidden
:slug: privacy-policy

I use privacy-first `Cloduflare Web Analytics <https://www.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/>`_
to gather anonymous statistics and learn which articles are the most popular
among the website visitors.

Cloudflare Web Analytics does not use any client-side state, such as cookies or
localStorage, to collect usage metrics. It also doesn’t “fingerprint” individuals
via their IP address, User Agent string, or any other data for the purpose of
displaying analytics.
